Engineered for High-Density Traffic Intelligence

From raw roadside camera footage to structured forensic telemetry. Every metric is computed strictly from real detector output — zero fabrication.

UVH-26 YOLOv11-S Detection

Fine-tuned specifically for dense heterogeneous traffic. Detects cars, two-wheelers, auto-rickshaws, buses, and trucks with high precision even in crowded lanes.

ByteTrack Persistent Tracking

Associates bounding boxes across frames using Kalman filters. Eliminates duplicate counts from temporary occlusions, ensuring rock-solid unique vehicle counts.

Traffic Density & Flow Analysis

Calculates real-time traffic congestion levels (Low, Medium, High, Severe) and computes vehicle flow velocities to pinpoint traffic bottlenecks.

Automated Class Normalization

Standardizes 14 granular training categories (hatchback, sedan, SUV, van, three-wheeler, etc.) into clean actionable operational categories.

Live Subprocess Streaming

Watch model inference execute frame-by-frame. Server-Sent Events (SSE) stream stdout logs into a sleek embedded console terminal in real time.

Forensic Reports & History

Every run generates a persistent JSON report and web-optimized MP4. Review past sessions, compare traffic patterns across dates, and download one-click JSON audits.

End-to-End Processing Pipeline

Four seamless stages turn raw video files into actionable surveillance intelligence.

STAGE 01
Video Ingestion

STAGE 02
AI Inference

A second pass renders high-definition bounding boxes, trajectory trails, and web-ready MP4 encoding.

Actionable Telemetry

Inspect analytics dashboards, replay annotated footage, and download full forensic JSON reports.
